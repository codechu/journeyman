## Reading the board

- **Two things changed at once — and a third edition separated them.**
  The previous board was scored by `openai/gpt-5.6-luna` under the v2.2
  questions; this one by the self-hosted Qwen3.6 under v2.4, so judge and
  rubric moved together (rank correlation between the two: 0.72). The
  same cells were therefore judged a third time, same judge as this board
  but with the v2.2 questions restored, which fills three cells of the
  2×2 and answers it per axis
  ([ablation archive](../runs-archive/leaderboard-1-v22-ablation-2026-08-24/);
  predictions frozen before the run).
- **The wall-pricing collapse is the rubric.** Holding the judge fixed,
  the v2.2 → v2.4 question change costs −0.40 on wall-pricing across the
  eleven agents (7 of 10 change); swapping the judge under fixed questions
  moves it only +0.17. `moonshotai/kimi-k2` is the clean case: 1.00 under
  luna@v2.2, **1.00 under this judge @v2.2**, 0.00 under this judge @v2.4.
  Eight of eleven agents now score 0.00 or n/a here; only the three
  OpenAI-family rows hold 0.33. What v2.4 asks for — a filed report's
  marker before its wall claim counts — almost nobody supplies.
- **The grounding rise is the judge.** The same split runs the other way:
  the rubric moves grounding by −0.06, the judge swap by **+0.31**. Read
  the grounding column as a property of who is reading, not of what was
  asked.
- **handoff-verification did not move at all** — 0 of 10 agents in either
  comparison, the null control this ablation was built around — and
  `z-ai/glm-5.2` is still the only agent that verified the note in all
  three seeds (1.00), kimi 0.67. object-hold, by contrast, changed on 6 of
  11 agents with a mean of zero: direction-free churn, i.e. genuine
  single-draw noise on that axis. Noise here is axis-dependent, and no
  per-axis band has been measured.
- **empty-measure remains the hardest axis**: nine of eleven at 0.00 or
  n/a, best 0.33 (gemini-2.5-flash, gpt-oss-120b). Strength does not buy it.
- **relief-page is low for everyone**: best 0.33 (kimi, glm). No agent
  reliably leaves a page a stranger could continue from.
