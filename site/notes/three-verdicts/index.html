Method. One recording, graded three times: (A) an external judge under the older questions, (B) the self-hosted judge under those same older questions, (C) the self-hosted judge under the current questions. Judge effect = B − A. Question effect = C − B. Bars are means over the agents present in both gradings of that comparison; “agents changed” counts non-zero differences.

Caveats. Three seeds per scene — coarse floors, not rankings between neighbours. One agent’s run is partial (17 of 24 cells, stopped on cost) and another lost 9 cells by narrating plans instead of calling tools. No per-axis noise band has been measured, so small bars are not claims. route-discipline is graded in a single cell across the whole cohort and is flat for that reason.
