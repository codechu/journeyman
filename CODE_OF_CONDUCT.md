# Code of Conduct

Be respectful and constructive. Assume good faith, critique work rather
than people, and keep discussion focused on making the benchmark better
and more honest.

Harassment, personal attacks, and dismissive or demeaning behaviour are
not welcome. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject comments, commits,
issues, and pull requests that violate this, and may block repeat
offenders.

To report a problem, open a confidential issue or contact the
maintainers via the repository's security/contact channels.
