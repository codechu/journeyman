## Reading the board

- **Strength and empty-measure run opposite.** The two strongest agents
  (glm-5.2, kimi-k2) score **0.00** on empty-measure: they keep assaying
  after the readings stopped informing. gpt-oss-120b — mid-table — reads
  1.00 on the same axis. Doing the job well and noticing when the job has
  gone empty are different muscles; this board is the first place that
  shows it across a cohort.
- **handoff-verification separates the top.** Only glm-5.2 verified the
  handoff note in all three seeds; kimi 0.67; everyone else 0.33 or worse.
  The night-watch trap fires on strong models too.
- **relief-page is near-zero for everyone** (best 0.33). No agent in this
  cohort reliably leaves a page a stranger could continue from — the
  axis is hard, not broken (the judge reads it at 1.0 on the exam).
- **route-discipline is `n/a` for every agent**: each hit the detour wall
  exactly once and moved on. The trap did not fire on this cohort — a
  scene-pressure note for the maintainers, not a score.
- **object-hold is at the ceiling for this cohort** — 1.00 for all nine
  cohort-1 agents; only the two small reference-run models sit below
  (gpt-oss-20b 0.33, mistral-small 0.67). A scene every strong agent
  aces is treated as suspect: the Finished Cart is flagged for a harder
  dial.
- **Invalid cells are a finding too.** mistral-small narrated plans
  instead of calling tools on 9 cells; the provider's 429 storms cost
  nemotron four cells on the first pass (re-run on resume). Both are
  visible in the `invalid` column rather than hidden.

## Caveats, plainly

Three seeds per scene — coarse floors, not rankings between neighbours.
The judge was the v1.3-qualified self-hosted Qwen3.6; on the later v2
exam it fell short on grounding (0.75), so these profiles are dated to
the v1.3 era and will be re-judged under v2 (cell records are kept).
The judge is a Qwen family member, so the `qwen/qwen3.6-35b-a3b` row
carries that affinity. `deepseek/deepseek-r1-0528` is partial (17/24):
its reasoning tokens on 40-85-call maze cells cost more than the rest
of the cohort combined, and the maintainer stopped it. All sealed
reports, with per-seed values and costs, are under
[`runs-archive/leaderboard-1-2026-08-23/`](../runs-archive/leaderboard-1-2026-08-23/).
